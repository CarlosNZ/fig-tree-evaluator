## `getCatalog()`

The call, and what it returns: `categories` (only those in use), `operators`, `fragments`, `literal` and `issues`. What each field of an entry means, including `nullOutcome`, `seed` and `sample`, and `metadata` as the definition's merged with the listing's, the listing's winning on a clash. Writing a listing for your own operator is on Presenting your operator, which this links to rather than repeats.

## Fragments

How a fragment appears in the catalog: its definition's `listing`, with any maps merged over it, and `fragmentColors` for a fragment without colours of its own. Writing a fragment's listing is in Fragments in an editor, which this links to.

## Overriding categories

`categories`: changing a category's display name, order or colours.

## Type seeds

`typeSeeds`: the starting value for a new parameter of each type, and the rule `getCatalog()` uses to pick one for a parameter without a seed.

## Malformed input

What `issues` reports, and what's skipped: `getCatalog()` never throws over a malformed listing.

## The package's data

A short reference to what `fig-tree-evaluator/catalog` exports besides `getCatalog()`: `operatorListings` (including `literal`), `categoryListings`, `fragmentColors` and `typeSeeds`.
