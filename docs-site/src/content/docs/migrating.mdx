## At a glance

Same package, version 3. A clean break, with a converter for stored expressions. Requirements: ESM only, and Node 22.12 or later.

## The steps

Update the package, convert your expressions, review the converter's report, then fix by hand what it flags.

## Updating host code

`FigTreeEvaluator` becomes `FigTree`, and the options and methods that changed: `evaluateExpression()` becomes `evaluate()`, the removed methods and what replaces them, and each v2 option and its v3 replacement. HTTP and SQL: I/O operators registered with a client, and the renamed SQL connections. Links to HTTP and SQL.

## Converting expressions

`migrateV2Expression()`: converting one expression, what it takes and what it returns. `migrateV2Fragments()`: converting a v2 instance's fragments. Running the converter over a store of expressions in bulk, and checking its output, including comparing v2 and v3 results for the same inputs.

## The converter's report

The issues it returns: `non-convertible`, `intentional-semantic-change` and `lossy-default`, and what to do with each. What it can't rewrite, and why. The changes it makes without raising an issue.

### Custom functions

v2's `CUSTOM_FUNCTIONS` become custom operators. The wrapper recipe for registering an existing function, and checking each converted call.

## What's changed

### Operators

Each v2 operator and what it is in v3. Generated from the operators' definitions.

### Recycled names

`!`, `get`, `lower`, `join`, `data` and `convert` mean something different in v3. The converter handles them; anyone reading or editing by hand needs to know.

### Results that differ on purpose

No implicit type coercion, different null handling, evaluation of the whole expression by default, and `updateOptions()` merging `data` and `fragments`.
