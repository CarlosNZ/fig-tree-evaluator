## `defineOperator()`

What it checks, and why every registered operator must go through it.

## Registering

Adding it to the `operators` option alongside `coreOperators`. Kept short: choosing and leaving out operators, `operatorSet` included, is covered in [Choosing operators](/guides/setup/#choosing-operators).

## What the engine does for you

Type checks, null handling, defaults, truthiness and `fallback` all happen before or after the body runs, so the body holds only the operator's own logic. The checks on what the body returns: `undefined` becomes `null`, a result that isn't a finite number fails (`non-finite-result`), and returning a lazy handle instead of its value is an error.
